Dr. Raghavendra Kulkarni provides expert diagnosis and advanced treatment for a broad spectrum of urological conditions affecting the urinary tract and male reproductive system. With years of specialized experience in modern urology, he focuses on delivering personalized, evidence-based care using advanced technology and minimally invasive techniques whenever possible.

His patient-centered approach ensures that every individual receives detailed evaluation, accurate diagnosis, and treatment tailored to their specific condition, symptoms, and lifestyle needs.

## Laparoscopic Urology

Advanced minimally invasive laparoscopic procedures performed for various urological conditions, offering smaller incisions, reduced pain, quicker recovery, and shorter hospital stay.

## Reconstructive Urology

Specialized surgical treatments aimed at restoring normal urinary tract function and improving quality of life in patients with structural or functional urological problems.

## Urinary Tract Infections (UTI)

Comprehensive treatment for uncomplicated and complicated urinary tract infections with accurate diagnosis, targeted therapy, and preventive guidance to reduce recurrence.

## Overactive Bladder

Evaluation and management of symptoms such as urinary urgency, frequent urination, and loss of bladder control using personalized medical and lifestyle-based treatment approaches.

## Haematuria

Detailed assessment and treatment for blood in urine, including identification of underlying causes through advanced diagnostic evaluation and appropriate management.

## Lower Urinary Tract Symptoms (LUTS)

Expert care for urinary difficulties such as weak urine flow, incomplete bladder emptying, urinary frequency, and nighttime urination.

## Testicular Pain & Swelling

Comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for testicular pain, swelling, infections, torsion, and related conditions with timely medical and surgical management when required.

## Penile Prosthesis

Advanced surgical solutions for erectile dysfunction through penile prosthesis procedures aimed at restoring confidence, function, and quality of life.

Dr. Kulkarni is committed to providing ethical, compassionate, and advanced urology care with a strong emphasis on patient comfort, safety, and long-term well-being.
