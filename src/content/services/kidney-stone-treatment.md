Dr. Raghavendra Kulkarni offers advanced and minimally invasive treatment solutions for kidney stones and urinary tract stones. With extensive expertise in Laser and Endoscopic Stone Surgery, he provides effective stone management focused on accurate diagnosis, complete stone clearance, reduced discomfort, and faster recovery.

Using modern technology and personalized treatment planning, Dr. Kulkarni carefully evaluates the size, location, and complexity of the stones to recommend the most suitable treatment option for each patient. His approach emphasizes minimally invasive techniques that help patients return to normal activities quickly with minimal pain and shorter hospital stays.

## Treatments Offered

### RIRS (Retrograde Intrarenal Surgery)

A highly advanced minimally invasive procedure performed using a flexible endoscope and laser technology to remove kidney stones without any external cuts or incisions. RIRS is especially effective for stones located inside the kidney and offers faster recovery with minimal discomfort.

### PCNL (Percutaneous Nephrolithotomy)

An advanced procedure used for treating large or complex kidney stones through a small incision in the back. PCNL allows efficient removal of larger stones with excellent outcomes and reduced recovery time.

### MiniPerc & UltraMiniPerc

Modern minimally invasive variations of PCNL performed using smaller instruments for reduced pain, less bleeding, and quicker healing while effectively treating kidney stones.

### Laser Stone Surgery

Advanced laser technology used to precisely break kidney and urinary tract stones into smaller fragments for safe and effective removal. Laser stone treatment offers high precision with minimal damage to surrounding tissues.

### Endoscopic Stone Removal

Minimally invasive endoscopic procedures used to diagnose and remove stones from the urinary tract without large incisions, ensuring improved patient comfort and faster recovery.

Dr. Kulkarni is committed to providing safe, advanced, and patient-focused kidney stone treatment with the goal of preventing recurrence, preserving kidney health, and improving overall quality of life.
