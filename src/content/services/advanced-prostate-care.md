Dr. Raghavendra Kulkarni offers comprehensive and advanced treatment solutions for enlarged prostate (BPH) and associated urinary symptoms. With expertise in modern minimally invasive and laser-based procedures, he focuses on providing effective relief, improved urinary function, reduced discomfort, and faster recovery for patients.

Using advanced technology and personalized treatment planning, Dr. Kulkarni carefully evaluates each patient’s condition and recommends the most suitable treatment approach based on age, symptoms, prostate size, and overall health.

## Treatments Offered

### ThuFLEP Laser Therapy

A highly advanced laser procedure used to remove enlarged prostate tissue with precision. ThuFLEP helps improve urinary flow, minimizes bleeding, and offers quicker recovery with shorter hospital stay.

### Bipolar TURP

A modern endoscopic procedure for treating prostate enlargement using bipolar energy technology. This technique effectively relieves urinary obstruction with improved safety and faster healing.

### REZUM Water Vapour Therapy

A minimally invasive treatment that uses controlled water vapour therapy to reduce enlarged prostate tissue. REZUM is designed to improve urinary symptoms while preserving quality of life and reducing recovery time.

### Minimally Invasive Surgical Therapy (MIST)

Advanced minimally invasive procedures performed to treat prostate enlargement with less pain, minimal bleeding, shorter hospitalization, and quicker return to daily activities.

Dr. Kulkarni’s goal is to help patients regain comfort, confidence, and better urinary health through safe, effective, and evidence-based prostate care.
